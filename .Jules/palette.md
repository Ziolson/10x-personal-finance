## 2026-02-01 - Enhanced Empty States
**Learning:** Empty states are often the first thing a user sees on a new screen. A simple text message is functional but lacks emotional connection or guidance. Adding a relevant icon and a clear call to action (or explanation of how to get data) significantly improves the perceived quality of the app and guides the user.
**Action:** When creating list views, always include a rich empty state with an icon (muted color) and helpful text, rather than just "No items found".
